DURBAR COLLEZIONI
Expanded luxury portal

This is a full-stack Sites / Cloudflare Worker application, not a static HTML upload.
The source bundle includes the entire 81-style catalogue, all earlier assets,
new campaign assets, local fonts, API routes, database schema and migrations.

Hosted features
- ChatGPT sign-in supplied by Sites; user-owned profiles, orders and applications.
- Owner administration authorised server-side for muchato@gmail.com.
- D1 tables: profiles, inventory, orders, applications, settings, inquiries.
- R2 product imagery upload. Customer photos are NEVER accepted by an API.
- Server-side price, stock-status, variant, trade-account and quantity validation.
- Demo orders only; no cards, payment, fulfilment or inventory reservation.
- Pre-orders require a date and customer acceptance.
- Affiliate applications, approved links, order attribution and review ledger.
- Big & Tall, Aisha, Elizabethan, Mickey, swim, winter, suits, gold and Western.
- Customer portrait composition runs in browser memory; all photos clear on
  leaving or after 10 minutes. No automated content classifier or AI fitting.
- Media room, editorial motion film, house enquiries, configurable social links.

Owner workflow
1. Open My account and complete the adult/privacy acceptance.
2. Open House administration (also in footer for owner).
3. Inventory: edit or add style, upload image, set sizes/colours, USD price,
   inventory quantity, season, status and available date. Save.
4. Partners: review Retailer/Affiliate applications. Approval is required before
   trade pricing or an active affiliate reference can be used.
5. Orders: change status; review affiliate ledger after completion + 30 days.
6. Media & social: add actual channel URLs and verified press articles.

Before taking live payments
- Select commerce/payments provider and tax/shipping/returns integrations.
- Add atomic per-variant stock reservation, fulfilment, invoices, transactional
  email, refund/chargeback events, fraud controls and production monitoring.
- Replace demo dates/prices and publish physical sample measurements/materials.
- Upgrade trade price lists, partner payment terms and affiliate payout process.
- Publish reviewed consumer/privacy policies and jurisdiction-specific notices.
- For cloud AI try-on, choose a contracted provider; add real moderation, consent
  checks, fail-closed child handling and tested deletion across every processor.
  Do not forward customer photos through the current product-image upload API.
- Configure durbarcollezioni.com through hosting and the domain registrar.
  No custom domain is connected to this Sites project as of this delivery.

Verification
TypeScript and production build; commerce calculations and availability/access
helpers; complete image-reference audit. Interactive browser QA was unavailable
in this environment, so a final device checkout test is required before public
launch. Current Site audience is preserved; this release does not make it public.

Development
Use the supported Sites installer/build workflow. .openai/hosting.json preserves
the project identity and declares DB and BUCKET. Generated migrations are
schema-only. Credentials and runtime secrets are not included.
